PROJECT OVERVIEW: SoftSettle
What It Does:
SoftSettle is a decentralized micro-credit system for AI agents that enables low-trust, high-speed transactions using:

Off-chain state channels (Nitrolite) for fast micro-transactions
ENS-based reputation (credit scores stored on-chain)
Circle/Arc for USDC payouts when deficits occur
Smart contracts for session management and settlement
The workflow: Register agent → Lock collateral → Stream micro-payments off-chain → Close & settle on-chain with payout if needed.
